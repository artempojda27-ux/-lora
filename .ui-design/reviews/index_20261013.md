# Design Review: elora-deploy/index.html

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-13 · **Focus:** visual design, usability (e-commerce, responsive)
**Goal:** maximally premium look (Hermès × Apple) with all product photos on one ivory background.

## Summary
The palette, typography pairing (Cormorant Garamond + Inter) and motion are already premium. What still reads
"mass-market" is the product grid: loud orange sale pills, bold prices, a row of size boxes and an orange
scarcity line under every card, white boxed photo frames with shadows, and coloured accent bars in the trust strip.
Issues found: 9 — Major 4, Minor 3, Suggestions 2.

## Major
1. **Grid noise** (cards): size boxes + "Залишилось N розміри" under each card. Luxury grids show name and
   price only → move sizes to the hover quick-add and the product sheet.
2. **Sale treatment**: orange filled "Sale" pill and red-ish old price. → small caps text label, muted old price.
3. **Photo frame**: white box + drop shadow fights the unified ivory photo background. → frame takes the photo
   background colour, no shadow, near-square corners, hover zoom only.
4. **Price weight**: 600 weight prices look like a discounter. → regular weight, tabular numerals.

## Minor
5. Trust strip uses four white cards with orange bars → hairline-separated editorial columns.
6. Card title 14px/500 sans → 13.5px regular, more line-height; brand line in small caps above it.
7. Grid gutters 40/20px → 56/28px desktop for breathing room.

## Suggestions
8. Editorial "Maison" intro line between departments and catalogue.
9. Department tiles: products already on ivory — use contain + ivory, not cover-crop.

## Positive observations
- Consistent tokens on :root, reduced-motion support, spring easing, glass header.
- Mobile bottom sheets and filters sheet follow iOS patterns.

## Next steps
Implemented in the same change: 1–7, 9.
